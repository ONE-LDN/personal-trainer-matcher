## CRAIG CLOUT
**Role:** Osteopath & Strength and Conditioning Coach  
**Tier:** Senior

**Top specialisms:**
- Injury rehabilitation
- Health management and longevity
- Injury prevention within performance

**Who Craig works best with:**
Driven professionals who take their training seriously but are currently limited by pain, health issues, stiffness or inconsistent progress. Clients who want to train properly, move well and perform at a high level without recurring setbacks. They want a structured, informed approach that covers all their needs — testing, nutrition, programming outside of sessions, and hands-on clinical support. The combination of osteopathy and strength coaching helps them resolve issues and build a body that performs both in and out of the gym.

**Who Craig is NOT for:**
Weight loss clients or low-need clients. Craig's specific skillset is clinical and performance-focused — clients who simply want to lose weight or have straightforward goals would be paying a premium rate for expertise they don't actually need.

**Best result Craig delivers:**
Helping clients return to sport, break through perceived limits, and level up their performance without fear of injury.

**One-liner:** Return to sport, break through perceived limits and perform without fear of injury. Clinical diagnosis meets progressive strength training.
